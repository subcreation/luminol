# Luminol Roadmap

Status: accepted behavior; v0.1 public release in preparation

## Promise

Luminol keeps an expectation/reality gap visible long enough to establish its
mechanism. It favors evidence over a fast-sounding patch and a later independent
or live exercise over premature resolution.

## How To Read This Roadmap

- `[x]` means the capability and its witness are accepted.
- `[~]` means a working candidate exists, but an integrated witness, review,
  release gate, or unresolved Mystery remains.
- `[ ]` means implementation remains.

The bold title is the Story. Packaging work appears only inside the Story it
enables. This roadmap adds no routing authority, workflow state, or permission.

## Roadmap

- [x] **An agent investigates an expectation/reality gap by evidence before
  editing production code.**

  **Detail:** The accepted behavior freezes a reproducible expected/observed
  witness, keeps competing hypotheses with named discriminators, runs the
  smallest discriminating test, preserves one deterministic red reproduction,
  changes the proven shared seam, and marks CANDIDATE then RESOLVED only on
  later independent or live non-recurrence. The linked philosophy remains at
  `references/philosophy.md`. This is not a state machine, retry loop, or
  permission system.

  **Witness:** The frozen `SKILL.md` and philosophy have guided Current Mystery
  investigations without premature resolution and survived direct human review
  without reopening accepted behavior. No adaptive-rigor or measured-survival
  result is claimed.

- [~] **People can install the same Luminol behavior independently of Current.**

  **Detail:** The standalone package reproduces the frozen skill (with
  internal team names in its ownership section replaced by generic roles) and
  the linked philosophy byte-for-byte, and supplies one onramp for Codex,
  Claude Code, and Grok Build, licensing, documentation, final hero artwork,
  and clean lifecycle exercises. Independent review and the tagged public
  release remain.

  **Witness:** A fresh reviewer installs each supported host, verifies both
  exact artifacts and their link, walks one frozen Mystery to CANDIDATE, removes
  it without residue, and confirms a public tag matches the accepted artifacts.

- [ ] **People can judge Luminol against a reproducible Mystery baseline.**

  **Detail:** Publish frozen Mysteries with known mechanism, run under no skill,
  generic debugging guidance, and exact Luminol.

  **Witness:** Public fixtures, classification rules, and raw runs report
  candidate fixes that survive independent retest beside attempts before
  correction, discriminator yield, Mystery bounce, recurrence, regressions, and
  human rescue.

- [ ] **Current imports an exact Luminol release without silently drifting.**

  **Detail:** Current consumes one tagged skill and its philosophy reference
  through a reviewed manifest and deterministic sync path, not a floating
  branch.

  **Witness:** A controlled update reproduces both tagged blobs byte-for-byte,
  shows the intended diff before review, and fails closed on a missing
  philosophy link or unexpected drift.

- [ ] **Investigation rigor calibrates from the observable Mystery record.**

  **Detail:** Post-release work begins with the least investigation able to
  establish cause, escalates after contradiction, recurrence, or failed
  candidates, and downshifts or partitions when further evidence changes no
  hypothesis or decision. Adaptive rigor, decision yield, off-ramps, and
  leaderboards are proposals, not present behavior.

  **Witness:** Matched Mystery work shows calibration in both directions with
  no premature resolution and no hidden recurrence.

## Domain Scope And Specialization

The first release is developed for software Mysteries: repository evidence,
process trees, logs, controlled fixtures, and production seams. Its candidate
shared core is preserving a contradiction and testing its mechanism. Future
specializations must prove which domain witnesses matter without turning
Luminol into Throughline title grammar or loading unnecessary process into every
worker.

## How Progress Is Judged

Primary measure: **candidate fixes that survive independent reality-based
retest**.

Frozen Mysteries with known mechanisms are scored first for no premature
RESOLVED state, no hidden recurrence, and no weakened expectation. A quiet run
with no exposure is unmeasured, not stable. Only then are candidate survival,
attempts, discriminator yield, Mystery bounce, recurrence, regressions, human
rescue, turns, tokens, cost, and time recorded. No efficacy percentage or
adaptive-rigor measure is claimed; see [benchmarks/README.md](./benchmarks/README.md).

## Non-Goals

- Throughline Story or Mystery title grammar.
- A parser, state machine, retry policy, permission layer, or workflow engine.
- Declaring a plausible patch resolved before independent or live non-recurrence.
- Adaptive (lighter or deeper) investigation rigor as current behavior.
- Decision-yield, off-ramp, leaderboard, or efficacy claims before a
  reproducible benchmark.

## Contributing

Useful contributions bring a frozen Mystery, literal witness, competing
hypotheses, a smallest discriminator, and a reproducible result. Evidence that
does not change a hypothesis or decision is not enough. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Direction Status

The v0.1 public release preserves accepted Luminol behavior and its linked
philosophy. It does not add adaptive-rigor behavior or make a
measured-survival claim.
